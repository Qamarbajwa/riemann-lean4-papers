This small pull request adds Finset.sum_Ico_eq_zero_of_palindromic_neg to Mathlib/Algebra/BigOperators/Intervals.lean.

When a finite sequence on Finset.Ico 0 L is odd under reflection m -> L - 1 - m, proving that the interval sum vanishes requires manually pairing terms via Finset.sum_Ico_reflect or constructing a bijection each time. Adding this helper lemma handles that standard reflection cancellation in one line.

Here is how the proof works step by step:

Step 1: Write S = sum_{m in Ico 0 L} f m.
Step 2: Reindex the finite sum using the involution m -> L - 1 - m on Ico 0 L, which gives
  S = sum_{m in Ico 0 L} f (L - 1 - m).
Step 3: Apply the hypothesis f (L - 1 - m) = -f m inside every summand to obtain S = -S.
Step 4: In any ordered or linearly additive torsion-free abelian group (or ring of characteristic zero such as R), S = -S implies S + S = 0 and therefore S = 0.
