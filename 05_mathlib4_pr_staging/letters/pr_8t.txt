In the earlier periodicity push, we proved that the modular Nyman cell residual satisfies the palindromic reflection rule A_c(L - 1 - m) = -A_c(m) across any common multiple period L of {1, ..., N} whenever D1 = 0 and D0 = 2. We can picture this geometrically as two cones joined in opposite directions: as the index m moves forward from 1 toward L - 2, the reflected index L - 1 - m moves backward in the opposite direction with the opposite algebraic sign.

This single push formalizes what happens when we fold the L2 cell quadrature sum and the signed flux across these two opposite cones on the interior interval 1 <= m <= L - 2. First, because (-A_c(m))^2 = A_c(m)^2, the forward cone and the opposite negative cone carry the exact same squared amplitude at every step. Second, when we take the relative signed flux A_c(m) * (A_c(m) - A_c(L - 1 - m)) between the two opposite cones, the minus sign in A_c(L - 1 - m) = -A_c(m) turns subtraction into addition and doubles the squared amplitude to 2 * A_c(m)^2. Third, by pairing each forward cell m of width 1 / (m * (m + 1)) with its counter moving mirror cell L - 1 - m of width 1 / ((L - 1 - m) * (L - m)), the partial L2 quadrature sum up to L - 2 folds cleanly into the average of the wide mouth cell width and the narrow tip cell width.

The step by step maths behind how this works is very simple:

Step 1: For any index m with 1 <= m <= L - 2, let A_c(m) = modularCellResidual c N m. By palindromic reflection on D1 = 0 and D0 = 2, the counter moving index L - 1 - m satisfies
A_c(L - 1 - m) = -A_c(m).

Step 2: Square both sides to get the energy symmetry between the two cones:
(A_c(L - 1 - m))^2 = (-A_c(m))^2 = (A_c(m))^2.

Step 3: Compute the unweighted counter rotating signed flux between the forward value A_c(m) and the backward value A_c(L - 1 - m):
A_c(m) * (A_c(m) - A_c(L - 1 - m)) = A_c(m) * (A_c(m) - (-A_c(m))) = 2 * (A_c(m))^2.
Summing this from m = 1 to L - 2 gives twice the total squared residual sum across the interior period.

Step 4: Now include the harmonic cell widths w(m) = 1 / (m * (m + 1)) and w(L - 1 - m) = 1 / ((L - 1 - m) * (L - m)). In the width weighted counter rotating flux, substituting A_c(L - 1 - m) = -A_c(m) gives
A_c(m) * (A_c(m) * w(m) - A_c(L - 1 - m) * w(L - 1 - m))
= (A_c(m))^2 * (w(m) + w(L - 1 - m))
= 2 * (A_c(m))^2 * ((w(m) + w(L - 1 - m)) / 2).

Step 5: As m runs forward from 1 to L - 2, the mirror index L - 1 - m runs backward through the exact same set of integers {1, ..., L - 2}. Using the reflection sum identity on Icc 1 (L - 2) together with Step 2, we have
sum_{m=1}^{L-2} (A_c(m))^2 * w(L - 1 - m) = sum_{m=1}^{L-2} (A_c(L - 1 - m))^2 * w(L - 1 - m) = sum_{j=1}^{L-2} (A_c(j))^2 * w(j).

Step 6: Adding the forward L2 sum and the reflected L2 sum and dividing by 2 folds the partial L2 quadrature sum harmonicCellL2PartialSum c N (L - 2) into the symmetric double cone kernel:
harmonicCellL2PartialSum c N (L - 2) = sum_{m=1}^{L-2} (A_c(m))^2 * ((w(m) + w(L - 1 - m)) / 2),
and the total weighted counter rotating flux across 1 <= m <= L - 2 equals 2 * harmonicCellL2PartialSum c N (L - 2).
