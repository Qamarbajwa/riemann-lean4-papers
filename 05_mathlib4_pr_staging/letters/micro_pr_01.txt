This small pull request adds one helper lemma Nat.mod_sub_one_sub_add_mod to Mathlib/Data/Nat/ModEq.lean.

Right now, when reflecting an index m modulo k inside a full period k * q with m < k * q, users have to expand natural subtraction inside Finset sums or split cases manually because (k * q - 1 - m) % k is not related directly to m % k in the library. This small addition closes that gap directly.

Here is the school-style proof step by step:

Step 1: Write m in Euclidean form as m = k * (m / k) + m % k, where 0 <= m % k < k.
Step 2: Since m < k * q and m >= k * (m / k), it follows that m / k + 1 <= q and m % k <= k - 1.
Step 3: Rewrite the reflected index as
  k * q - 1 - m = k * (q - (m / k) - 1) + (k - 1 - m % k).
Step 4: Since 0 <= k - 1 - m % k < k, taking remainder mod k drops the multiple of k cleanly:
  (k * q - 1 - m) % k = k - 1 - m % k.
Step 5: Adding m % k to both sides gives (k * q - 1 - m) % k + m % k = k - 1.
