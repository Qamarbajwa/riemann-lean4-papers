## Overview
Companion micro-paper documenting the intuitive, visual, and experimental genesis behind the five-paper Lean 4 formalization suite (**Papers A–E**):
1. **Visual Exploration (`Section 1`):** Explains why spatial cone embeddings (`zeta_bicone_unfolding_and_dual_sheet.py`) and prime–composite Dirichlet partitions (`composite_zeta_and_prime_duality.py`, 50/50 equilibrium horizon $s^* \approx 1.44653762$) guided the search for formal invariants.

3. **Analytic Obstructions & Necessity of Lean 4 (`Section 3`):** Documents superlinear Gram ill-conditioning ($\kappa(G_{64}) = 11{,}555.80$), rank-4 boundary amplification ($\det(U_4)^{-1} \approx 664.38\times$), and why formal verification in Lean 4 is essential to catch subtle algebraic mismatches.
4. **Traceability Architecture (`Section 4`):** Maps every geometric insight cleanly to **Papers A, B, C, D, and E** and Wave 1 Mathlib PR staging (`MATHLIB4_PR_STAGING/`).
