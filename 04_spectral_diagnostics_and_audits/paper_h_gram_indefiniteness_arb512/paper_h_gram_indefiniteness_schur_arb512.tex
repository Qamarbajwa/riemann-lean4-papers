\begin{abstract}
Two distinct finite-section obstruction mechanisms govern computational studies of the Riemann Hypothesis: (1) slow logarithmic $O(1/\ln N)$ decay combined with kernel indefiniteness in rational-dilation B\'aez-Duarte (BNBD) approximations, and (2) severe Rayleigh--Ritz ill-conditioning and interval-wrapping pathologies in smooth non-dilation Legendre truncations of Weil's quadratic functional on $L^2([-L, L])$. Neither body of certified computational evidence appeared in Papers~0 or~A--E. This paper consolidates the verified computational and algebraic findings from five audited Python and \texttt{flint.arb} modules ($39$ passing automated tests). On the BNBD side, $50$-digit and $192$-bit \texttt{flint.arb} spectra prove that isolated $G_{\mathrm{smooth}}$ ($24/50$ negative eigenvalues at $N = 50$) and isolated $G_{\mathrm{cot}}$ ($2/50$ negative eigenvalues, $\lambda_{\min} = -9.8002$ on \texttt{omp\_plus\_swap\_50}) are both indefinite, while greedy cardinality compression cuts $d_S^2$ to $0.00973769$ ($2.33\times$ compression) yet stays strictly above the B\'aez-Duarte--Balazard--Landreau--Saias (BBLR) floor $0.00850088$. On the boundary-locked subspace $\mathcal{A}_N$ ($N \le 100$), $100$-digit KKT verification matches $1 + Q_N^{(\mathrm{cot})}$ to $1.474 \times 10^{-110}$. On the Weil operator side ($L = 1, T = 400$), $\lambda_{\min}(H^{(N)})$ plunges from $1.8157 \times 10^{-4}$ ($N=4$) to $4.3430 \times 10^{-26}$ ($N=50$), and $512$-bit \texttt{flint.arb} unwrapped congruence resolves both historical negative witnesses in $P_{650}$ as IEEE \texttt{float64} roundoff and $11.96\times$ interval box wrapping, certifying $12/12$ positive $LDL^T$ pivots across $750$-mode true-integral $L = 1$ Schur elimination. In accordance with \texttt{NO\_RH\_PROOF = TRUE} and \texttt{FULL\_POINT\_L1 = NOT\_ISSUED}, these finite-section and banded certificates do not assert full infinite-operator Weil positivity.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Vasyunin Gram matrix, Nyman--Beurling sparse compression, Weil positivity finite sections, \texttt{flint.arb} interval arithmetic, Schur complement wrapping, $LDL^T$ certification.

\section{Introduction and Epistemic Scope}

Empirical and rigorous-numeric probes of the Nyman--Beurling--B\'aez-Duarte (BNBD) space and Weil's explicit-formula quadratic form encounter opposite numerical bottlenecks. Consecutive rational dilations $\rho_k(x) = \{1/(kx)\}$ remain moderately conditioned at low $N$ ($d_{50}^2 = 0.01165042$, $\lambda_{\min}(G_{50}) = +4.3481 \times 10^{-4}$), yet converge slowly at the logarithmic BBLR barrier $O(1/\ln N)$~\cite{BaezDuarte2000}. Conversely, shifted Legendre bases on $L^2([-L, L])$ suppress high-frequency leakage rapidly but suffer super-exponential eigenvalue decay across $N \le 50$, exposing finite-precision and interval-arithmetic computations to catastrophic cancellation and box-wrapping false negatives.

\paragraph{Epistemic Demarcation (\texttt{NO\_RH\_PROOF = TRUE}).}
All results reported here govern verified finite-dimensional subspaces, boundary-locked algebraic identities, or banded exterior coercivity diagnostics ($39$ passing tests across seven test suites). Following repository certification gates, full continuum operator positivity remains open (\texttt{FULL\_POINT\_L1 = NOT\_ISSUED}), and no resolution of the Riemann Hypothesis is claimed.

\section{Boundary-Locked Affine KKT Verification ($N = 2 \dots 100$)}\label{sec:boundary_kkt}

Evaluating the boundary-locked affine KKT optimum and two-sparse corrector $w_{\mathrm{corr}}^{(N)}$ across $N \in \{2, \dots, 100\}$ at $100$-digit \texttt{mpmath} and $192$-bit \texttt{flint.arb} (\texttt{boundary\_\allowbreak locked\_\allowbreak cotangent\_\allowbreak study.py}) confirms all five invariants to sub-$10^{-100}$ accuracy:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Maximum identity defect $|\mathcal{E}_N - (1 + Q_{\mathrm{cot}})|$: $1.4736 \times 10^{-110}$; affine constraint residuals: $2.0943 \times 10^{-115}$ ($D_1$) and $2.2841 \times 10^{-115}$ ($D_0 - 2$); KKT gradient residual: $9.4212 \times 10^{-116}$.
  \item $192$-bit \texttt{flint.arb} Gram and cotangent radii: $\le 2.3473 \times 10^{-56}$ and $\le 1.1149 \times 10^{-104}$.
  \item At $N = 100$, constrained affine optimality pays a penalty of only $2.2665 \times 10^{-6}$ over unconstrained BNBD ($E_{\mathrm{aff},\min} = 0.01020419$ vs.\ $d_{\mathrm{NB}}^2 = 0.01020192$ and $d_{\mathrm{unc}}^2 = 0.01002779$), while $N\|w_{\mathrm{corr}}^{(N)}\|_2^2 = 1.229040$ approaches $\ln(2\pi) - \gamma = 1.260661$ within $0.031621$.
\end{itemize}

Across $N \in [10, 200]$ in \texttt{constructive\_\allowbreak affine\_\allowbreak cotangent\_\allowbreak study.py} ($80$-dps, $\max|\mathcal{E}_N - (1+Q_{\mathrm{cot}})| = 3.795 \times 10^{-80}$), the decoupled \texttt{affineLock} family~D decays monotonically from $\mathcal{E}_{10} = 0.032638$ to $\mathcal{E}_{200} = 0.011180$ ($\mathcal{E}_{200}\ln 200 = 0.05924$), with Bettin--Conrey decomposition isolating a stabilizing smooth-canceling channel ($-1.971813$) against a bounded reciprocity remainder ($+0.982993$).

\section{Non-Dilation Legendre/Weil Operators and 512-Bit Schur Diagnosis}\label{sec:weil_schur}

In \texttt{non\_\allowbreak dilation\_\allowbreak hilbert\_\allowbreak comparison.py}, the shifted Legendre/Weil operator $H^{(N)}(L, T)$ ($L = 1.0, T = 400$) matches independent $4$-mode pilot receipts to $1.090 \times 10^{-18}$ and maintains $256$-bit \texttt{flint.arb} quadrature-doubling stability at $N = 50$ ($3{,}904$ vs.\ $7{,}808$ nodes) within $5.316 \times 10^{-43}$ ($\operatorname{rad} \le 7.639 \times 10^{-58}$). Across $N \in \{4, \dots, 50\}$, every finite truncation exhibits zero negative eigenvalues (\texttt{neg\_even = neg\_odd = 0}), while $\lambda_{\min}(H^{(N)})$ decays over $21$ orders of magnitude: $1.8157 \times 10^{-4}$ ($N=4$, $\kappa = 2.370 \times 10^3$), $8.4106 \times 10^{-8}$ ($N=8$), $9.5320 \times 10^{-13}$ ($N=16$), $3.9712 \times 10^{-20}$ ($N=30$), $1.0221 \times 10^{-23}$ ($N=40$), and $4.3430 \times 10^{-26}$ ($N=50$, $\kappa = 9.413 \times 10^{25}$).

\subsection{Resolution of Two Frozen \texttt{p650} / Critical-Subspace False Negatives}
Auditing the $650 \times 650$ assembly and $12 \times 12$ critical subspace (\texttt{certify\_\allowbreak p650\_\allowbreak and\_\allowbreak critical\_\allowbreak schur\_\allowbreak arb512.py}) resolves two historical obstructions:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*),noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Roundoff \& Interval-Box Wrapping in \texttt{p650\_\allowbreak stable\_\allowbreak assembly}:} True $256$-bit \texttt{flint.arb} midpoints on \texttt{FINITE\_LOWER} ($+1.8160 \times 10^{-15}$) and \texttt{SCHUR\_LOWER} ($+4.3186 \times 10^{-16}$) are strictly positive; earlier negative signs ($-1.7460 \times 10^{-15}$ and $-2.0846 \times 10^{-15}$) were IEEE \texttt{float64} artifacts. Moreover, entrywise interval wrapping inflated the box penalty by $11.96\times$ (to $2.6464 \times 10^{-15}$), pushing the worst box corner negative ($-2.2146 \times 10^{-15}$). Rewriting via the exact \textbf{Quadratic Residual Schur Congruence Identity},
  \begin{equation}
  z^T S z = \begin{pmatrix} z \\ -w \end{pmatrix}^T \begin{pmatrix} A_{CC} & A_{CB} \\ A_{BC} & A_{BB} \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} z \\ -w \end{pmatrix} - r^T A_{BB}^{-1} r, \qquad r = A_{BB}w - A_{BC}z,
  \end{equation}
  removes wrapping inflation and certifies $z_{\mathrm{schur}}^T S z_{\mathrm{schur}}/\|z_{\mathrm{schur}}\|^2 > +2.10 \times 10^{-16} > 0$.
  \item \textbf{$12/12$ Critical Schur $LDL^T$ Positivity:} Subtracting the true $512$-bit bulk solve action $G_{\mathrm{bulk}} = X^T M_{\mathrm{bulk}}^{-1} X$ (error radius $\pm 8.15 \times 10^{-183}$) and quadratic solve penalty $\delta_{\mathrm{quad}} \le 6.6 \times 10^{-326}$ (in place of the uniform scalar penalty $1.1876 \times 10^{-23}$, which exceeded the smallest eigenvalue $9.6096 \times 10^{-30}$) certifies \textbf{$12/12$ strictly positive pivots} (min even pivot $+2.5371 \times 10^{-22}$, min odd pivot $+1.6826 \times 10^{-20}$).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{512-Bit Exterior Coupling, Banded $[650, 749]$ Coercivity, and $750$-Mode Closure}
In \texttt{certify\_\allowbreak l1\_\allowbreak exterior\_\allowbreak schur\_\allowbreak coupling\_\allowbreak arb512.py}, decomposing exterior leakage $Q_{650}(K_{400} + V + W)\widetilde{Z}_{\mathrm{crit}}$ on the $738$-mode elimination space $X_{\mathrm{bulk}} \oplus P_{[650, 749]}$ yields:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Pole and Archimedean Channels ($c = 0.20$):} Schur penalties $\le 2.1048 \times 10^{-3875}$ (pole) and $\le 2.1772 \times 10^{-116}$ (directed Bessel $j_n$), $71$--$86$ orders of magnitude below $\lambda_{\min}(S_{\mathrm{crit}}) = 5.2420 \times 10^{-30}$.
  \item \textbf{Two-Tier Banded Coercivity ($[650, 749]$ + $Q_{750}$):} Direct $512$-bit \texttt{GL750} + $50 \times 50$ interval $LDL^T$ on $[650, 749]$ certifies $100/100$ positive pivots and coercivity $\lambda_{\min} \ge 3.521050 > 0.90$, lowering the $Q_{750}$ critical threshold from $c_{\mathrm{crit}}^* = 0.211054$ to $c_{\mathrm{tail},750}^* = 0.191801 < 0.20$.
  \item \textbf{End-to-End $750 \times 750$ True-Integral $L = 1$ Schur Enclosure:} Incorporating exact analytic $32$-node quadrature remainder $\epsilon_{32} \approx 2.5428 \times 10^{-32}$ and $c_{\mathrm{tail},750} = 0.20$ preserves $12/12$ strictly positive $LDL^T$ pivots ($\min_k \underline{D}_k = +2.3269 \times 10^{-22}$).
\end{itemize}
